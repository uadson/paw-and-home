## Essential Buyer's Guide

Before ordering on Amazon, evaluate these critical factors:

### 1. Measure Drawer Depth Twice Before Ordering
Most standard American kitchen drawers measure between 17 and 21 inches deep. Check your drawer's inner dimensions to ensure the divider's minimum retracted length fits inside.

### 2. Check End-Pad Quality
Dividers exert constant outward tension. Look for thick EVA foam or soft rubber pads that cushion against drawer joints and prevent cosmetic denting.
