## 3 Essential Tips for Measuring Under-Sink Spaces

Before clicking order on Amazon, grab a measuring tape and check these key dimensions:

1. **Plumbing Clearance:** Measure from the cabinet floor to the lowest curve of your P-trap pipe. Ensure your organizer's top tier sits lower or offset from this pipe.
2. **Door Hinge Inset:** European-style concealed cabinet hinges often stick out 1 to 1.5 inches into the opening. Make sure your organizer doesn't scrape against the hinge when pulled out.
3. **Disposal Vibration Space:** Garbage disposal motors vibrate slightly when running. Leave at least 1 inch of buffer between the motor casing and your organizer frame.
