## Buyer's Guide: What to Look for Before Purchasing

When shopping for an automated pet drinking fountain on Amazon, consider these four vital buying criteria:

### 1. Material: Stainless Steel vs. Ceramic vs. Plastic
* **Stainless Steel (Recommended):** Surgical grade 304 or 316 steel is scratch-resistant, antibacterial, and completely dishwasher safe. It effectively eliminates "feline chin acne" (black spots caused by clogged pores).
* **Ceramic:** Heavyweight and naturally cool. Ideal if you have aggressive drinkers who knock lightweight bowls around.
* **Plastic:** Inexpensive, but tiny micro-scratches from cleaning brushes can harbor bacteria over time. Always ensure it is certified BPA-free.

### 2. Pump Noise & Decibels
Cats have sensitive hearing tuned to high frequencies. A buzzing or grinding motor can frighten nervous felines away from drinking. Look for submersible DC motors rated under **25 decibels** (roughly equivalent to a quiet library whisper).

### 3. Filtration Architecture
A reliable fountain uses dual or quadruple-stage filtration:
* **High-density PP cotton:** Catches stray fur, food particles, and debris.
* **Activated coconut shell carbon:** Removes chlorine, bad odors, and tap water tastes.
* **Ion exchange resin:** Softens hard tap water by reducing calcium and magnesium, which helps minimize bladder stones.
