## Essential Buyer's Guide

Before ordering on Amazon, evaluate these critical factors:

### 1. Snout Shape Compatibility
Deep labyrinth ridges work wonderfully for long-snouted dogs (Retrievers, Shepherds). For flat-faced dogs, choose shallow undulating bumps so they do not injure their facial folds.
