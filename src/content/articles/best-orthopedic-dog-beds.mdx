## Essential Buyer's Guide

Before ordering on Amazon, evaluate these critical factors:

### 1. Foam Quality: Shredded Foam vs. Solid Memory Foam
Cheap pet beds often use recycled shredded foam scraps that shift and create low spots within weeks. Always look for solid block memory foam or high-density orthopedic egg-crate foam that retains its structural thickness.

### 2. Waterproof Liners are Non-Negotiable
Memory foam cannot be run through a washing machine without disintegrating. A high-quality bed must feature a zippered TPU or PU waterproof membrane between the outer fleece and inner foam.

### 3. Measuring Your Dog Correctly
Measure your dog from the tip of the nose to the base of the tail while sleeping stretched out, then add 6 to 10 inches to ensure their spine remains aligned without their head dangling over the floor.
